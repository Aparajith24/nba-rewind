# Moment candidates

The playoff moments the app will ship with, 1996–97 through 2025–26. More can be added later.

Scores and clocks here come from memory and news recaps. **Each one gets verified against play-by-play** before its moment file is built.

**Type** sets how much game the sim has to replay:
- **Last shot**: one possession, under ~10 seconds.
- **Clutch**: the final minute, a few possessions.
- **Takeover**: several minutes or a whole overtime.
